**Keenroll.** "Keen" means sharp, and "roll" is how it works. It's short, easy to say on camera ("link's in bio, Keenroll"), and spells the way it sounds.

## Tagline

**"Sharp knives. Zero skill."**

Alternates for testing: "Roll it. Slice it. Done." · "The 1-minute knife fix." · "Your knives, back to day one."

## Voice

Warm, plain-spoken, and a little cheeky, like a friend who cooks a lot. We **show**, we don't hype.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Roll it 20 times. Slice a tomato. That's it." | "REVOLUTIONARY sharpening technology!!!" |
| "Most people never sharpen their knives because it's a pain. This isn't." | "Professional chefs HATE this trick" |
| "If it doesn't work for you, send it back within 60 days." | Fake scarcity, fake reviews, "only 3 left!" |
| Real numbers: "15° for Japanese knives, 20° for Western ones." | Health or income claims |

## Customer avatar

**Primary — "Gift-buying Gina" (Q4)**
- 30–55, buying for a dad, husband, or the home cook who "has everything." Budget $40–80.
- Pain: running out of gift ideas. Wants something useful that *looks* considered.
- Objections: "Will they actually use it?" "Will it arrive by Christmas?" "Is it cheap junk?"
- What convinces her: the paper-slice demo, a nice gift box, clear holiday cutoff dates, and an easy return.

**Secondary — "Home-cook Hank" (evergreen)**
- 28–60, cooks 4+ nights a week, owns a decent knife set that's gone dull. Tried a pull-through sharpener (chewed his edge) or owns a whetstone he never uses.
- Pain: squashed tomatoes, slipping onions, "I'll get them sharpened someday."
- Objections: "Will it damage my good knives?" "Is it harder than it looks?" "Why not a $10 pull-through?"
- What convinces him: the angle explanation (fixed 15°/20° vs. pull-through grinding), a before/after on *his* kind of knife, and it taking about a minute.

**Who it's NOT for:** serrated knives, scissors, single-bevel Japanese knives (yanagiba/deba). We say so on the page, which cuts returns.

| Offer | Price | Per kit | Landed cost* | Contribution before ads** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Kit** | **$59** | $59 | $19.00 | **$35.13** |

| **3 Kits** (family gifting) | **$129** | $43 | $46.00 | **$72.30** |
| Replacement disc set (post-purchase) | $19 | | ~$5 | ~$12 |

\* Landed = product + shipping (multi-kit orders ship together, so per-unit shipping drops). Product $13, single ship $6, 2-pack ship $7, 3-pack ship $7.
\** After landed cost, Shopify Payments (2.9% + $0.30), $0.50 packaging per kit, and a 4% refund reserve.

- **Shipping:** **Free US shipping on everything.** It's baked into the price. (With a $59 entry price, a free-shipping threshold just adds friction. Free shipping beats "free over $X" for conversion.)
- **Savings shown honestly:** "Save $20" on the 2-pack = 2 × $59 − $98. No inflated compare-at prices.
- **No permanent "sale."** Run real promos only on real dates (BFCM, a launch week), and end them when you say you will.
