Our 60-day "sharp or it's free" guarantee

If your Keenroll doesn't sharpen your non-serrated kitchen knives, contact us within 60 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price.

How to request a refund

  1. Email [EMAIL] with your order number and a sentence about what went wrong. A photo or short video helps us improve.
  2. For most guarantee claims we'll refund you without asking you to send it back. If we do need the item returned (for example, for a suspected defect or on large or repeat orders), we'll email you a return address and cover return shipping for defective items.
  3. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days after approval.

Changed your mind?

Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the product price. Unless the item is defective, return shipping is paid by you, and we recommend a tracked service. Please email us before sending anything. Packages sent without contacting us first may not be processed.

Damaged, defective, or wrong item

Email a photo within 14 days of delivery and we'll send a free replacement or a full refund (your choice). There's no need to return it.

Exceptions

  • Damage from misuse (for example, sharpening serrated or ceramic knives, or dropping the discs on hard floors) isn't covered by the guarantee, though we'll always try to help.
  • Gift cards and replacement disc sets that have been used are non-refundable.

Cancellations

You can cancel within 12 hours of ordering for a full refund, as long as your order hasn't shipped.
