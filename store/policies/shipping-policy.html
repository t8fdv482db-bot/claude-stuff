We want you to know exactly when your Keenroll will arrive, before you buy.

Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in the United States (all 50 states, plus APO/FPO/DPO). We don't ship internationally yet.

Cost

Standard shipping is free on every US order.

Delivery times

  • Processing: 1–3 business days (Mon–Fri, excluding US holidays) to prepare and hand your order to the carrier.
  • Transit: 7–12 business days to most US addresses. Our products ship from our fulfillment partner's warehouses, which can be overseas. When an item ships from a US warehouse, transit is typically 3–6 business days.
  • Typical total: 8–15 business days from order to delivery.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Peak season (mid-November to December), weather, and carrier delays can add a few days. We publish holiday order-by dates on the product page. Those dates are based on the maximum transit time above.

Tracking

You'll get an email with a tracking number when your order ships. Tracking can show "no information" for 2–4 days while the label is handed off. You can check status any time on our Track Order page.

Multiple items

Orders with more than one kit may arrive in separate packages, each with its own tracking.

Lost, late, or damaged packages

  • If tracking hasn't updated in 10 business days, or your order hasn't arrived 20 business days after purchase, contact us. We'll investigate with the carrier and, if it's lost, send a replacement or a full refund (your choice).
  • If your order arrives damaged, email a photo within 14 days of delivery and we'll replace it free.
  • If tracking shows "delivered" but you can't find it, check with neighbors and your local post office, then contact us within 7 days.

Wrong address

Please double-check your address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us within 12 hours of ordering and we'll do our best to fix it before it ships.
