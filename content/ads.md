# Paid Ads Playbook (for when organic starts working)

**Don't run paid ads until:** (1) you have at least one organic video above 10K views with a >50% 3s hold rate, (2) your store has converted at least a few organic visitors, and (3) the TikTok Pixel + Events API and the Meta Pixel + Conversions API are installed (Shopify's TikTok and Facebook & Instagram channels do this in a few clicks).

**Start with Spark Ads (TikTok) / partnership or boosted posts (Meta).** Promote the organic post itself, which keeps its likes and comments as social proof.

**Budget plan:** $30–50/day for 5–7 days on 3–5 creatives. Kill any creative with CPA above $35 after ~$70 spend. Scale winners 20–30% every 2–3 days. **Break-even CPA ≈ $35** on a single kit and higher with bundles (see `/brand/brand.md`).

**Policy guardrails:** kitchen framing only. No knives pointed at the viewer. No "weapon" language ("blade," "razor" are fine in a cooking context). No fake urgency, no unverifiable claims ("#1," "best-selling," "chefs' choice"), no before/after on people, no other brand names.

## Angle 1: "The 1-minute fix" (demo / problem-solution). Start here.

**Audience:** broad, 25–65, US, interests: cooking, home improvement, kitchen. Or let TikTok/Advantage+ target automatically.
**Creative:** Script 1 (Tomato Test) or Script 2 (Paper ASMR). The first 2 seconds must be the squash or the slice.

**Primary text (Meta) / caption (TikTok):**
> Your knife isn't bad. It's dull. 🍅
> Stick it to the magnet, roll 20 times, flip, repeat. The angle is locked in at 15° or 20°, so there's nothing to get wrong.
> ✓ Diamond + ceramic discs ✓ No water, no power ✓ Free US shipping ✓ 60-day "sharp or it's free" guarantee

**Headline:** Dull to sharp in about a minute
**CTA button:** Shop Now

**Variations to test:**
- "Stop buying new knives. Fix the ones you have."
- "The tomato test never lies."

## Angle 3: "Will it ruin my good knives?" (objection-busting / education)

**Audience:** retarget video viewers (50%+) and site visitors from the last 30 days. Also broad cooking enthusiasts.
**Creative:** Script 8 (15° vs 20°) or Script 7 (green-screen a real skeptical comment).

**Primary text:**
> "Won't a gadget sharpener wreck my chef's knife?" Fair question.
> Pull-through sharpeners drag your edge through a fixed carbide V. Keenroll holds your knife at a fixed 15° (Japanese-style) or 20° (Western) and you roll a diamond disc along the edge. Start with a few rolls and check. You're in control.
> Not for serrated or single-bevel knives. We'll tell you straight.
> Free US shipping · 60-day guarantee

**Headline:** Sharpen your good knives, the right way
**CTA:** Learn More → product page FAQ anchor (`/products/keenroll-rolling-knife-sharpener#faq`)

## Measurement

| Metric | Healthy target | If off… |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (3s views / impressions) | > 30% (paid) | New first 2 seconds |
| CTR (link) | > 1% | Stronger offer or CTA in the video |
| Landing page CVR | > 2% | Fix page speed, photos, price clarity |
| CPA | < $25 (target), < $35 (break-even) | Kill the creative / test a new angle |
| AOV | > $70 | Push the 2-Kit bundle harder |

## Retention (free money after the first sale)

- Post-purchase email at day 3: how-to video + safety tips
- Day 14 (after delivery): honest review request through Judge.me. **Never offer incentives conditional on a positive review.** Discounts for leaving *any* review are allowed only if disclosed, and must not depend on the rating.
- Day 60: "Time for a touch-up?" + replacement disc set ($19)
- Q4: "Gift one" email to past buyers with the 2-Kit offer
