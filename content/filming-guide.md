# Filming Guide: Shoot 10 Videos in One Afternoon

## 1. What to order as samples (today)

| Item | Qty | Why | Cost est. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rolling sharpener kit, **AliExpress backup listing** | 2 | Fastest to arrive. One for filming, one to check consistency | ~$32 |
| Rolling sharpener kit, **CJ sourced version** | 1 | Compare quality before picking the primary | ~$15 |
| Cheap **dull** knives from a thrift store or Dollar Tree | 4–6 | Your "before" footage. Thrift-store knives are perfectly dull | ~$10 |
| One decent chef's knife (if you don't own one) | 1 | Aspirational "good knife" shots | $20–40 |
| Tomatoes, onions, printer paper, carrots | | Test props, every shoot | ~$10 |
| Plain kraft gift box + twine/bow | 3 | Gift/unboxing footage until branded boxes exist | ~$10 |

**While you wait for samples (7–15 days):** film talking-head hooks, education (15° vs 20°), dull-knife "before" clips, and tomato-squash shots now. Edit them into the demo footage when the sharpener arrives.

## 2. Phone and lighting setup (no budget)

- **Phone:** any phone from the last 4 years. Shoot **4K 30fps** for normal clips and **120/240fps slow-mo** for slices. Clean the lens.
- **Light:** face a **window** with daylight on the product (best and free). Avoid overhead kitchen lights (yellow, harsh shadows). If filming at night, a $20–30 LED panel or ring light at 45° to the side, 5600K.
- **Background:** wood cutting board on a clean counter. Warm and simple. Remove clutter and other brands' logos.
- **Stabilize:** a $15 phone tripod with an overhead arm for top-down shots. A stack of books works too.
- **Audio (very important for ASMR):** turn off fans, fridge hum, and music. Clip a lav mic or put the phone mic close to the roller for the "zzzt" sound. Record 10 seconds of room silence for editing.
- **Focus:** tap and hold to lock focus and exposure (AE/AF lock) on the blade before each take.
- **Angles:** (1) top-down, (2) 45° hero angle, (3) eye-level macro on the edge, (4) POV from your chest.

## 3. B-roll shot list (film all of these, reuse forever)

- [ ] Magnet "clack" as the knife snaps onto the block (5 takes, best sound)
- [ ] Rolling: wide, macro on disc, top-down, slow-mo
- [ ] Flipping the knife to the other side
- [ ] Swapping to the ceramic side
- [ ] Knife held **upside down** by the magnet (the "wow" shot)
- [ ] Tomato squash (before), dull knife
- [ ] Tomato slice (after), real time + slow-mo
- [ ] Paper slice: hanging sheet, curls, top-down
- [ ] Onion skin slide (before) vs clean cut (after)
- [ ] Macro of the edge before and after (phone macro mode or a $10 clip-on lens)
- [ ] Hands lifting the lid off the box
- [ ] Product beauty shots: roller standing, side by side with the block, on a wood board with herbs
- [ ] Prep montage: julienne, chiffonade, dicing (sharp knife)
- [ ] Wrapping it as a gift
- [ ] Wiping the discs with a cloth (care)

## 4. Product photos for the store (needed before ads)

Shoot on the same afternoon, horizontal **and** square, 3000px+:
1. Hero: roller + block + knife on a cream background (match the store's `#F6F1E9`)
2. What's in the box: flat lay
3. In use: hands, rolling
4. Close-up: diamond disc vs ceramic disc
5. Scale: in hand
6. Gift box
7. A 2-pack shot for the bundle option

Upload these in Shopify → Product → Media. The theme hides the "illustration" note automatically once media exists.

## 5. Safety and policy checklist for every video

- Fingers away from the edge, cutting away from your body
- Never point a knife at the camera or a person, no "stunts," no tossing
- Frame it as a **kitchen tool**: food, cooking, care
- No other brands' names or logos in frame
- No medical/health or income claims
- People on camera have given consent

## 6. Batch plan (one 3-hour session gives 10 videos)

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Set up light, tripod, clean counter, prep props |
| 0:20–1:20 | All B-roll from the list above |
| 1:20–2:00 | Scripts 1, 2, 5, 9, 14 (demo-heavy, mostly B-roll) |
| 2:00–2:40 | Scripts 3, 4, 8, 13 (talking + demo) |
| 2:40–3:00 | Store product photos |

Edit in **CapCut** (free): auto captions on, captions in the middle third (out of the UI's way), clips 0.5–2 seconds long, and the hook text on screen from frame 1.
