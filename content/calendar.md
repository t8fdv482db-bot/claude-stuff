# 14-Day Posting Calendar

**Cadence:** 2 posts a day on TikTok (12:00 and 19:00 local). Cross-post the same video to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts (remove the TikTok watermark by exporting from CapCut before uploading).
**Daily 20 minutes:** reply to every comment, ideally with video replies to good questions. Comment replies become your next videos.

Script numbers refer to `scripts.md`. Hook numbers refer to `hooks.md`.

**Before samples arrive (Days 1–3):** use pre-demo formats like the dull "before" footage, education, and founder story. Be honest in these videos that the demo is "coming when my samples land." Building anticipation is fine. Don't fake a demo.
