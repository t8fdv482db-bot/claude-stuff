# 15 Short-Form Video Scripts: Keenroll

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15–35 sec. Built for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
**Golden rules:**
1. **Payoff in the first 2 seconds.** Show the slice or the squash, not your face saying "hey guys."
2. **Sound matters.** The roll "zzzzt" and the paper slice "shhk" are your hooks. Record clean audio (see filming guide).
3. **Safety on camera, always.** Cut away from your body, keep fingers off the edge, never point a knife at the lens or at people. This keeps ad accounts safe too.
4. **Honesty rules (FTC + platform):** You own the brand, so never pose as a random customer. In "TikTok made me buy it"-style formats, make it clear it's your brand ("I made…" / "our…"). Paid creators must use the platform's paid-partnership toggle and #ad. Only react to **real** comments. No "only 3 left."
5. CTA: "Link in bio" (organic) / "Tap Shop Now" (ads). Once TikTok Shop is set up, use the product link.

## 1. The Tomato Test (problem → solution) ⭐ film first
- **Hook (0–2s):** Close-up: dull knife **squashes** a tomato. Juice everywhere.
- **On-screen text:** "My 'good' knife did this 😩"
- **Shots:** (1) squash, macro. (2) knife onto magnet block, *click*. (3) 3 sec of fast rolling. (4) flip, roll. (5) **same tomato**, paper-thin slice falls over. (6) slow-mo second slice.
- **VO:** "This is my expensive chef's knife. Embarrassing. Magnet. Twenty rolls. Flip. Twenty rolls… and that's what it's supposed to do."
- **Text at payoff:** "≈1 minute. Zero skill."
- **CTA:** "It's called Keenroll. Link's in bio."

## 2. Paper Slice ASMR (satisfying demo)
- **Hook:** Knife glides through a hanging sheet of printer paper. No talking, just sound.
- **On-screen text:** "the sound 🤤"
- **Shots:** paper slice (top-down) → rolling, macro on the disc → magnet *clack* → three more paper slices in spirals.
- **VO:** none (ASMR). Optional whisper at the end: "keenroll."
- **CTA text:** "Sharpener's in my bio"

## 7. Green-screen: Reacting to a REAL comment

- **On-screen text:** "fair question. let's test it"

- **VO:** "Fair. Here's the dullest knife in my house. No cuts, real time."

## 8. Objection Buster: "Won't it ruin my good knives?"

- **VO:** "A pull-through drags your edge through a carbide V. This holds a fixed angle you choose: 15 for thin Japanese knives, 20 for German ones. Start with fewer rolls, check, add more."
- **CTA:** "Questions? Comment and I'll answer on camera."
