# 30 Hooks to A/B Test

Use each hook as the **first 2 seconds**: on-screen text plus the matching visual. Keep everything after the hook the same so you're only testing the hook. Track 3-second view rate and average watch time in TikTok analytics. Winners have a 3s view rate above ~60%.

## Visual / sound-first (no talking)
1. *[Dull knife squashes tomato]* "my 'good' knife did this 😩"
2. *[Knife slices hanging paper]* "the sound 🤤"
3. *[Magnet holds knife upside down]* "wait… how is it holding that?"
4. *[Macro of shiny dull edge]* "this shiny line = your knife is dull"
5. *[Stopwatch at 0:00 next to dull knife]* "how fast can I fix this?"
6. *[Slow-mo tomato slice falling]* "this is what a sharp knife is supposed to do"
7. *[Pile of knives dumped on counter]* "sharpening every knife in my drawer"

## Problem / pain
8. "If your knife slides off onion skin, watch this."
9. "Nobody told me dull knives are more dangerous than sharp ones."
10. "I paid $150 for a knife and let it get this dull."
11. "Stop buying new knives. Do this instead."
12. "When was the last time you sharpened your knives? Be honest."
13. "Your knife isn't bad. It's dull."

## Curiosity / education
14. "The 15° vs 20° thing nobody explains."
15. "Why pull-through sharpeners are ruining your edge."
16. "Chefs sharpen at a fixed angle. Here's the cheat code."
17. "This little block is the whole trick."
18. "Whetstones scared me. This didn't."

## Gift / seasonal (Nov–Dec)
19. "The gift for the dad who has everything."
20. "Gifts for people who cook, under $60."
21. "Gave my dad the sharpener TikTok won't shut up about."
22. "The only kitchen gift that gets used every week."
23. "Last-minute gift that doesn't look last-minute."

## Social proof / challenge (truthful only)
24. "Does this actually work or is it a gimmick? Let's test it."
25. "Sharpen the worst knife in your house: the challenge."
26. "Real time. No cuts. Watch."
27. "You asked me to sharpen [comment's knife]. Okay." *(only real requests)*

## POV / relatable
28. "POV: you finally sharpened your knives after 6 years."
29. "POV: your partner tries to cut a tomato with your knives."
30. "Things that just make sense: 🧲"

### Test plan
- Week 1: post hooks **1, 2, 8, 19, 24** on the same base video (Script 1 footage). The highest 3-second hold rate wins.
- Week 2: test the next 5 against the week-1 winner.
- Keep a sheet with: hook # · video link · views · 3s rate · avg watch time · link clicks · orders.
