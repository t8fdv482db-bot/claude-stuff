## Spec to source (send this to every supplier)

- Solid wood roller (beech/acacia), 2 interchangeable discs: **diamond #400 or #600** (sharpen) + **ceramic #1000–3000** (hone). Extra diamond #1000 disc is a bonus.
- Magnetic angle block, **15° and 20°** (bonus: 12° / 18°). Magnet must hold an 8" chef's knife firmly.
- No third-party logos on the product. Plain or **custom laser logo (Keenroll)** once you order 100+.
- Retail box, plus an insert card (we'll design it).
- Packed weight under 600 g.

## Recommendation

1. **Today:** Order **samples** from the AliExpress backup listing (fast to get started, you'll need it for filming) **and** submit a **CJ sourcing request** with the spec above, asking specifically for "US warehouse stock option."
2. **Primary = CJ Dropshipping**, because of US warehouse potential for Q4, automated order sync, and branded packaging at scale.
3. **Backup = AliExpress via DSers.** Map the same variants so you can switch suppliers in minutes if CJ stocks out.
4. **Once you hit ~20 orders/day:** Pre-buy 300–500 units into CJ's US warehouse (or a 3PL) for 3–5 day delivery. Expect unit cost to drop to ~$7–9 with custom logo and box.
