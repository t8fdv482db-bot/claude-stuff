# Product Candidates — Research & Scoring

_Research date: 3 Nov 2026. Launch window: Q4 gifting season (BFCM → Christmas) + evergreen after._

## How I scored

Each criterion is 1–10 (10 = best). Max 70.

| Code | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **W** | "Wow" in the first 3 seconds (visible problem solved / satisfying demo) |
| **P** | Can sell at $30–$80 with ~3× markup on landed cost |
| **R** | *Not* easy to find cheap at Walmart/Target/Amazon (10 = hard to find) |
| **T** | Trend direction (growing, not peaked) + evergreen potential |
| **S** | Shipping & returns: light, small, unbreakable, no sizing |
| **Sup** | Supplier reliability (ratings, 7–12 day ship or US warehouse) |
| **L** | Legal / ad-platform safety (no batteries, liquids, health claims, kids' certs, IP risk) |

**Honest caveat on "R":** almost nothing that goes viral stays off Amazon for long. Copycats show up within weeks. So nearly every candidate scores low here. The real moat is **brand + offer + content**, not "nobody else sells it."

- Q4 guides: cold-weather items, home upgrades, giftables. Warning that heavy items lose money on freight ([Peregrine freight math](https://peregrineship.com/blog/q4-dropshipping-products/), [AutoDS Q4](https://www.autods.com/blog/q4-dropshipping-products/), [Copyfy Q4](https://www.copyfy.io/en/blog/best-dropshipping-products-q4))

- Rolling knife sharpener: Exploding Topics marks it "exploding," ~14.8K monthly searches ([Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/topic/rolling-knife-sharpener)). The Tumbler brand sells at $129 list (~$96 on sale) ([Tumblerware](https://www.tumblerware.com/products/tumbler-rolling-knife-sharpener), [FrontDeskReview Oct 2026](https://frontdeskreview.com/product/knife-sharpeners/tumbler-diamond-rolling-knife-sharpener/))
