# Keenroll Launch Checklist

Everything here is something **you** have to do by hand. The time estimates assume you're new to it.

## ⚡ Do these 3 things in the next hour

1. **Order samples (20 min).** Buy 2× of the [AliExpress backup listing](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256806161075020.html) (or the best-rated equivalent you see today) plus a few thrift-store knives. Sign up at [CJ Dropshipping](https://cjdropshipping.com) and submit a sourcing request with the spec in `research/suppliers.md`, asking for a US-warehouse option. *Samples are your bottleneck. Nothing else can start without them.*
2. **Lock the name (20 min).** Check **keenroll.com** at Namecheap/Porkbun (fallbacks: getkeenroll.com, keenroll.co) and search "KEENROLL" at [tmsearch.uspto.gov](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov) (Class 8 and 21). If both are clear, buy the domain and claim **@keenroll** on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.
3. **Post your first video (20 min).** Film the "dull knife squashes a tomato" clip and post Day 1 from `content/calendar.md` (honest founder intro + 15° vs 20° education). The algorithm needs time to learn your account, so start the clock today.

## Day 1–2: Foundation

| ✓ | Task | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Create a Shopify account (free trial → Basic plan) | 15 min | Use your brand email |
| ☐ | Connect your domain (Settings → Domains) | 15 min | DNS can take a few hours |
| ☐ | Set up a support email on your domain (Google Workspace or Zoho Mail free tier) | 20 min | hello@keenroll.com |
| ☐ | **Business basics:** decide sole proprietor vs LLC (an LLC in your state is ~$50–500). Get a free EIN from the IRS (10 min online) | 30–60 min | Not legal or tax advice. Ask a local accountant if unsure |
| ☐ | Business bank account (or a separate personal account to start) | 30 min | Keep store money separate from day 1 |
| ☐ | Sales tax: Shopify Tax auto-calculates. Register in your home state if required. You'll get alerts if you cross other states' thresholds | 20 min | Settings → Taxes and duties |
| ☐ | Payments: activate **Shopify Payments** plus PayPal, and turn on Shop Pay, Apple Pay, and Google Pay | 15 min | Needs your bank info + EIN/SSN |
